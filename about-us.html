About Us

People-first advice,
rooted in your goals.

Rooted Rupees was founded on a simple belief: financial advice should be honest, personal and free of conflict. We combine research-backed strategy with plain-language guidance so every client understands exactly what they're doing and why.

Our story

Rooted Rupees started with a small group of advisors who were tired of watching good people make financial decisions out of fear, confusion, or pressure from products they didn't need. We set out to build something different — an advisory that starts with listening, not selling.

Today we work with individuals and families across every stage of life, from a first salary to a comfortable retirement, always anchored to one question: what's genuinely best for you?
